# Toggle

Item type `toggle`. It is for an on or off choice the user sets, such as a switch or a yes or no.

## With the mouse

Click it to switch it on or off; Clear takes your choice back.

## What the agent receives

The agent receives on or off. Off is an answer; a switch you did not touch gives none.

It has no keys of its own beyond the standard ones in `SKILL.md`.
