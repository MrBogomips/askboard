# Row

Item type `row`. It is for 2 to 4 items side by side; a narrow board stacks them.

## With the mouse

It places two to four items side by side, and stacks them on a narrow board: use the items themselves.

## What the agent receives

Nothing of its own: each item inside it gives its own answer.

It has no keys of its own beyond the standard ones in `SKILL.md`.
