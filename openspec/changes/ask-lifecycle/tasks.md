# Tasks

Each task writes its tests first and verifies that they fail before it implements (the repository's TDD rule). "Check passes" means `bun run check` (typecheck, lint, `bun test` with at least 80% coverage, imports), with `GITHUB_TOKEN=$(gh auth token)` for the licence step. "Browser tests pass" means `packages/view/test/browser/` in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, with no CSP violation from the view, the board page or the console. Every task that touches answers, revisions or epochs includes a test that the failing case appends nothing and reports nothing the user did not send (NFR-10, AD-10).

Scope and order: group 0 sets the base, groups 1 to 5 the session side from the contract outwards, group 6 the board, group 7 the records and the end-to-end runs. Groups 2 and 6 may run in parallel once group 1 is done, since they meet only through the contract's sets and transcripts. The files shared with the tree-widget and gantt-widget changes are in design.md, Merge points.
