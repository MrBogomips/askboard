# As built: `answering-flow-guidance`

In code since `answering-flow-guidance` (Issue #168), mostly in `packages/view/src/shell/`, `strings/`, `styles/` and `icons/`: what the board does after an answer, how it tells the user, and what is shown beside the state; the `afterAnswer` setting crosses the contract, the session process and `board-host`.

- The ladder and the flow: `answerStateOf` and `ladderCountsOf` (`answer-state.ts`) give one state to each question for the card, the outline, the navigator and the counts; `afterAnswerOf` (`after-answer.ts`, pure) says `stay`, `move`, `footer`, `complete` or `send-all`; `createFlow` (`flow.ts`) runs it at the act with `nextOpenFrom` and `previousOpenFrom` (`board-view.ts`, to do first, postponed last) and the footer store of `BoardContext` (`QuestionFooter`, "Continue", `createHeldKeyGuard` against a held key). `primaryLabelOf` (`primary-label.ts`) names the primary ("Send answer", "Confirm", "Edit", "Send the note", "Send the change").
- The move: `measureBoardInset` (`measure-inset.ts`), `scrollFrames` and the smooth `revealAndFocus` (`reveal.ts`), the sticky tab strip, the arrival mark and the reduced-motion rule (`styles/motion.css`). The messages: `sentMessagesOf`, `commandSentMessagesOf` and the other builders (`flow-messages.ts`), `createDeliveredGate` (`delivered-messages.ts`, one message per 5 s from `receiptCountsOf`). The panel: `completionOf`, `receiptCountsOf` and `shownCompletionOf` (`completion.ts`), `CompletionPanel`, and `HeadingRequest.to` with `requestFocusTo` for focus on Send or on the panel. Prefill: `selectRecommended` returns a `PrefillBatch`, `prefillCountOf` and `undoPrefill` (`submit.ts`); `hasRecommendation` is gone. Icons: ten more lines in `icons/index.ts`, drawn by `QuestionMarker`, the finished tab and the panel.
- Hooks: `data-askboard-primary`, `-submit`, `-postpone`, `-prefill`, `-next-to-do`, `-delivery`, `-receipt` (task 2.1), and `-footer`, `-continue`, `-arrived`, `-tab-strip`, `-tab-done`, `-completion`, `-completion-heading`, `-all-ready`, `-prefilled`, `-undo`, `-marker` (now on the icon's svg).
- Strings: the labels of the answer, Send, the helper texts and the keys' "Previous to do" were renamed; `header.openPostponed`, `footer.*`, `completion.*`, `bar.allReady`, `bar.undoPrefill`, `areas.done`, `status.next`, `status.postponed`, `status.declined`, `status.changeSent`, `status.readyLost`, `status.delivered`, `status.areaDone`, `status.prefillUndone` and `settings.afterAnswer`, `settings.goToNext`, `settings.stayHere` were added; `bar.selectRecommended` is "Prefill recommended ({count})". The Italian of the new keys is marked "(to review)" in EXPERIENCE.md.
- Versions bound: configuration 1.2 (`afterAnswer`) and board protocol 1.11. Chunks over main at 46d3dcf7: `shell` 239,027 to 260,749 B, `board-page` 1,039,339 to 1,045,253 B, `icons` 6,710 to 8,837 B, re-recorded in `chunk-sizes.json`. Corrects `answering-flow-fixes.md` (Submit's focus), `approval-scale-ranking.md` and `form-widget.md` ("Select recommended" asks no gate), `icon-set.md` (the nine exports, the jump's arrow) and `board-keys.md` (`Shift+N`'s order and name).
