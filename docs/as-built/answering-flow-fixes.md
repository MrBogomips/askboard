# As built: `answering-flow-fixes`

In code since `answering-flow-fixes` (Issues #179 to #185), all in `packages/view/src/shell/` and `strings/`: where focus goes after an act, which question is in view, what a screen reader is told, the bar's helper text, one next-open control and the visible reason beside a disabled "Answer".

- Focus and scroll: `revealDelta` (pure) and `revealAndFocus` (`reveal.ts`) scroll a heading or a field clear of the sticky header and bottom bar before focusing it, and every heading move uses them (`useHeadingFocus`, the card's `focusHeadingOf`); `AnswerResult` (`revision-confirm.ts`: `sent`, `note-sent`, `held`, `released`, `note-held`, `note-released`, `needs-reason`; `AnswerOutcome = AnswerResult | undefined`, where `undefined` means blocked, nothing done) is what `pressAnswer` reports and what the card and `settleConfirmation` read; `BottomBar.onSubmitted` runs after Submit (or the replace dialog's send) has sent, and the board focuses the heading of the question in view, else the first; "Send and switch" returns focus to "Immediate".
- The question in view: `questionInView` (`in-view.ts`) and `useQuestionInView` (`use-question-in-view.ts`, on scroll and `focusin`, never at mount) set `draft.inView` in sections and tabs; `shownQuestionOf` (`board-view.ts`) and `DocumentUpdate.shownQuestion` give the question shown in one question per view when none is in view, and `carriesNewRestore` (`document-update.ts`) is the restore guard.
- Status messages: the store `createStatusMessages` (`status-messages.ts`: `announce`, listeners, descriptors read together in order), `StatusRegion` (`status-region.tsx`, rendered by `Board` from its first render), the builders `sentMessageOf` and `addedMessageOf` (`flow-messages.ts`), and `Senders.status` (`card-acts.ts`, optional, called on outcome `sent` only). The agent pill carries `data-askboard-agent-pill`.
- Controls: `fieldToFixOf` (`field-block.ts`) names the field that disables "Answer", shown as a `button-link` before it whose `id` is "Answer"'s `aria-describedby` target; `focusField` (`focus-field.ts`) selects the field's Tabs-layout tab, then reveals and focuses it. The navigator's jump is gone; the header's "Next open ↓" is the one control on every presentation.
- Strings: added `bar.submittedHelp` and `status.answerSent`, `status.answerSentPlain`, `status.noteSent`, `status.ready`, `status.submitted`, `status.prefilled`, `status.added`; removed `navigator.nextOpen`. Chunks (`chunk-sizes.json`): `shell` 223,806 to 231,027 B (+7,221 B, above the 6,000 B the design estimated), `board-page` 1,036,297 to 1,037,505 B (+1,208 B), measured against main with `icon-set`; no other chunk changed. No earlier note of `docs/as-built/` becomes wrong.
