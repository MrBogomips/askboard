The retry re-enqueues the payment, and without a deduplication key a crash between charge and acknowledgement could enqueue it twice. Fix B guarantees idempotency at the queue level.

ADR-012 Retry policy Active ADR
docs/adr/ADR-012-retry-policy.md · Decision · lines 18–29

"Bounded exponential backoff: base delay 2 s, factor 2, jitter ±20%, each wait capped at 5 minutes; at most 6 attempts then dead-letter queue and PAYMENT_RETRY_EXHAUSTED; re-enqueueing must be idempotent by retry key; a retry never changes amount, currency or customer."

Consequences: Prevents retry storm on downstream gateways while preserving strict debit auditability.
